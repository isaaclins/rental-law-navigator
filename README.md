# Clause & Effect: Rental Housing Law Navigator

Which housing rules apply to this apartment today, and what is about to change?

An AI system that reads public housing law (CA, NJ, MA and 10 cities), turns it into structured rule records with
verbatim citations, resolves any sample address to its jurisdiction stack, and answers at address level, as of any date.

Built for the RealPage challenge at the Hack-Nation 7th Global AI Hackathon (Oct 3-4, 2026).

> **Not legal advice.** Outputs summarize public law for a research prototype.
