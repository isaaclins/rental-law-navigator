FICTIONAL SAMPLE ORDINANCE, WRITTEN FOR THE CLAUSE & EFFECT DEMO. NOT A LAW OF THE CITY OF NEWARK.

CITY OF NEWARK, NEW JERSEY
ORDINANCE 6PSF-26-14

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE XIX (HOUSING) OF THE REVISED GENERAL ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF NEWARK BY ADDING
CHAPTER 19:6, "FAIR RENT-SETTING AND APPLICATION FEES"

WHEREAS, the Municipal Council finds that software which pools nonpublic rent and occupancy data from competing
landlords and recommends rents can raise rents above what a competitive market would set; and

WHEREAS, applicants for rental housing in the City often pay several screening fees for a single search, which
falls hardest on households with low incomes;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Municipal Council of the City of Newark, New Jersey, as follows:

Section 19:6-1. Definitions.
As used in this chapter:
"Algorithmic rent-setting device" means any software, service or system that uses nonpublic data about rents,
occupancy or lease terms supplied by two or more landlords to recommend, set or adjust the rent or other lease
terms for a dwelling unit.
"Application fee" means any fee, charge or deposit, however named, that a landlord or the landlord's agent
collects from a prospective tenant to process a rental application, including a fee for a credit or background
check.
"Dwelling unit" means any room or group of rooms in the City rented or offered for rent as a home.

Section 19:6-2. Algorithmic rent-setting prohibited.
No landlord of a building containing five or more dwelling units shall use an algorithmic rent-setting device to
set the rent for any dwelling unit in the City, and no person shall sell, license or provide such a device for that
purpose. This section does not prohibit the use of a report that publishes aggregated rent data and does not
recommend rents for specific units.

Section 19:6-3. Exemptions.
Section 19:6-2 does not apply to a building containing four or fewer dwelling units, or to a dwelling unit owned
and operated by the Newark Housing Authority.

Section 19:6-4. Application fees.
No landlord shall charge an application fee exceeding twenty-five dollars ($25.00) per adult applicant. A landlord
who charges an application fee shall give the applicant a written receipt and, within fourteen days after the
application is denied or withdrawn, shall refund any part of the fee that was not spent on a credit or background
check.

Section 19:6-5. Penalties.
A landlord who violates this chapter shall be liable for a civil penalty of up to two thousand dollars ($2,000.00)
for each violation. Each month in which an algorithmic rent-setting device is used for a dwelling unit constitutes
a separate violation. The Department of Economic and Housing Development shall enforce this chapter.

Section 19:6-6. Severability.
If any provision of this chapter is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

Section 19:6-7. Effective date.
This ordinance shall take effect on January 1, 2027.
